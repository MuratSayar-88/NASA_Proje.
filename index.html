About Us

We are a group of space enthusiasts studying at Uğur Schools in Edremit, Türkiye. We created SPHEREx Sky Motion Lab to make exploring the changing sky more accessible and engaging.

Our project brings together real SPHEREx observations when available and clearly labeled catalog-based motion visualizations. The one-year catalog-motion preview is separate from SPHEREx's mission timeline.
